Pancakes

Description

Pancakes are soft, fluffy breakfast cakes made from a simple batter. They are quick to prepare and taste delicious with fruit, honey, or maple syrup.

This recipe makes a small stack that is ideal for breakfast or an afternoon snack.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup all-purpose flour
  • 1 tablespoon sugar
  • 2 teaspoons baking powder
  • 1 pinch of salt
  • 1 egg
  • 3/4 cup milk
  • 2 tablespoons melted butter
  • Butter or oil, for the pan

Steps

  1. In a bowl, mix the flour, sugar, baking powder, and salt.
  2. In another bowl, whisk together the egg, milk, and melted butter.
  3. Pour the wet ingredients into the dry ingredients and stir until just combined. A few small lumps are fine.
  4. Heat a lightly greased pan over medium heat.
  5. Pour a small amount of batter into the pan for each pancake.
  6. Cook until bubbles appear on the surface, then flip and cook the other side until golden.
  7. Repeat with the remaining batter.
  8. Serve warm with your favorite toppings.
