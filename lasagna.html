Lasagna

Description

Lasagna is a comforting Italian-inspired dish made with layers of pasta, rich tomato sauce, creamy white sauce, and cheese. It is baked until the top is golden and bubbling.

This recipe is perfect for sharing with family or friends. Serve it hot with a fresh salad.

Ingredients

  • 9 lasagna sheets
  • 500 g ground beef
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 2 cups tomato sauce
  • 2 cups white sauce
  • 1 1/2 cups grated mozzarella cheese
  • Salt and pepper
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 180°C (350°F).
  2. Heat the olive oil in a pan and cook the onion until soft.
  3. Add the ground beef, season with salt and pepper, and cook until browned.
  4. Stir in the tomato sauce and simmer for 10 minutes.
  5. Spread a little meat sauce in a baking dish. Add a layer of lasagna sheets, meat sauce, white sauce, and cheese.
  6. Repeat the layers until the ingredients are used, finishing with white sauce and cheese.
  7. Bake for 30–40 minutes, until golden and bubbling.
  8. Let it rest for 10 minutes before serving.
