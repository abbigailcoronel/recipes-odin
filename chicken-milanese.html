Chicken Milanese

Description

Chicken Milanese is made with thin chicken cutlets coated in breadcrumbs and cooked until crisp and golden. It is a simple, satisfying meal that can be served with salad, mashed potatoes, or fries.

A squeeze of lemon just before eating adds a fresh flavor.

Ingredients

  • 2 chicken breasts
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 cup breadcrumbs
  • 1/2 cup all-purpose flour
  • 1 teaspoon garlic powder
  • Salt and pepper
  • Oil, for cooking
  • 1 lemon, cut into wedges

Steps

  1. Slice each chicken breast horizontally to make thin cutlets.
  2. Season the chicken with salt, pepper, and garlic powder.
  3. Place the flour, beaten eggs, and breadcrumbs in three separate shallow dishes.
  4. Coat each cutlet in flour, dip it in egg, and then cover it with breadcrumbs.
  5. Heat a thin layer of oil in a frying pan over medium heat.
  6. Cook the cutlets for about 3–4 minutes on each side, until golden and fully cooked.
  7. Place on a paper towel briefly to remove excess oil.
  8. Serve hot with lemon wedges and your favorite side dish.
